Life Territory

Conway's Game of Life meets territorial strategy.

How to play

Build: Draw live cells only on your territory. Use Buy area to spend Space on unclaimed cells.

Launch: Conway patterns are strategic stamps. A pattern must start on your land and costs Space.

Turn cycle: In multiplayer, players build in sequence. End your turn and the board advances the selected number of generations. You then receive 5 Space + 1 per 10 land cells.

Territory: Living cells claim the cell they occupy. A newborn cell takes the owner of the majority of its three parents.

Win: In multiplayer, reach 50% of the board or be the only player with territory. In solo mode, reach 60%.
